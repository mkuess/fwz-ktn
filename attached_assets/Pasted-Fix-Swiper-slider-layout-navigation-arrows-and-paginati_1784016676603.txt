Fix Swiper slider layout - navigation arrows and pagination dots should be OUTSIDE the cards, not inside them. Do NOT delete the database.

In resources/views/home.blade.php find the testimonials swiper section and fix the layout:

STEP 1 - Move navigation arrows outside the swiper-wrapper:
The swiper-button-prev and swiper-button-next must be siblings of swiper-wrapper, not inside slides.
The swiper-pagination must also be outside swiper-wrapper.

Correct structure:
<div class="swiper testimonials-swiper" style="position:relative;padding-bottom:3rem">
    <div class="swiper-wrapper">
        @foreach($testimonials as $testimonial)
        <div class="swiper-slide">
            <div class="testimonial-card" onclick="openTestimonialModal({{ $testimonial->id }})" style="cursor:pointer;height:100%">
                ... card content ...
            </div>
        </div>
        @endforeach
    </div>
    
    <!-- Pagination BELOW the slides -->
    <div class="swiper-pagination" style="bottom:0"></div>
    
    <!-- Arrows OUTSIDE cards, vertically centered on slides -->
    <div class="swiper-button-prev"></div>
    <div class="swiper-button-next"></div>
</div>

STEP 2 - Style the navigation arrows to match FWZ design:
Add to CSS (app.css or inline style block):
.testimonials-swiper .swiper-button-prev,
.testimonials-swiper .swiper-button-next {
    color: #1a2e1a;

}

Make all cards same height:
